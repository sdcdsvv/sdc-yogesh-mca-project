# Professional Email Templates for EduNexa LMS

## 🎨 Design System

### Color Palette:
- **Primary Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%)`
- **Text Primary:** `#1f2937`
- **Text Secondary:** `#4b5563`
- **Background:** `#ffffff`
- **Info Box:** `#f3f4f6` with `#667eea` border
- **Warning Box:** `#fef3c7` with `#f59e0b` border

### Typography:
- **Font Family:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto`
- **Heading:** `18px, 600 weight`
- **Body:** `15px, 1.8 line-height`
- **Small:** `13-14px`
